# Google Play store listing (English, Canada)

## App name

Mihiraki: Two-Page PDF Reader

## Short description

Read manga and scanned books in two-page spreads with a private PDF reader.

## Full description

Read manga, art books and scanned documents in a two-page spread. Mihiraki supports right-to-left reading and uses PDF metadata to detect the layout and reading direction when that information is available.

• Choose single-page or two-page viewing and adjust cover placement and reading direction.
• Use bookmarks and the table of contents to jump to chapters. Search PDFs with a text layer and see matches highlighted on the page.
• Read files on your device offline. Rendering and search take place on your device.
• Download English or French sample PDFs from the app. Browse by language and start with the first or latest episode.

Mihiraki is free and has no ads. Optional one-time purchases support development and award badges. All reading features are available without a purchase.
