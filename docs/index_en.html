MihirakiPDF

A modern PDF viewer optimized for two-page spreads and R2L reading on Android

Privacy Policy GitHub

✨ Key Features

📖 Seamless Spreads

View two pages side-by-side. Perfect for immersive manga and art book layouts.

🇯🇵 Native R2L Support

Full support for right-to-left reading order, including inverted sliders and intuitive swipe gestures.

🔄 Auto-Detection

Automatically detects page layout and reading direction from PDF metadata for a zero-config experience.

🔍 Precise Search

Fast text search with accurate hit highlighting (yellow background and red border) on documents.

🛡️ Privacy First

MihirakiPDF is designed with privacy at its core.

  • All PDF processing is performed locally on your device.
  • Your documents are never uploaded to any external server.
  • No unnecessary internet permissions are required.

*Note: Internet connection is only used when explicitly opening files from cloud storage services like Google Drive.

💰 Pricing

Free & Ad-Free

The app is completely free to use. All core features, such as PDF viewing, spreads, and search, are available without any restrictions regardless of purchase.

*Optional in-app tips are available to support the developer. Supporters will see a special commemorative badge in the Settings screen.
