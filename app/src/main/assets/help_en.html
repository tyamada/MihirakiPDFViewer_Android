Open PDF: Use the file picker to select a PDF file from your device or cloud storage.

Sample PDFs: Browse, filter by language, download, and instantly open sample manga and documents provided by the app.

Menu: Tap the screen to toggle the visibility of the toolbar and slider.

Navigate Pages: Switch pages by swiping, using the slider, or clicking the left/right edges (on desktop/mouse navigation mode).

PDF Table of Contents (Outline): Tap the table of contents icon in the top toolbar to view document bookmarks/chapters and instantly jump to any section.

Search: Enter text into the search bar to find specific content within the PDF.

Zoom: Pinch to zoom in or out. Long-press and drag to scroll while zoomed in.

Adaptive Layout: Automatically optimized for tablets, large screens, and foldables.

High quality: Render PDF pages in higher quality.

Sharpness: Render text and lines more sharply.

Two-page view: Display two pages side-by-side.

Show cover: Display the first page standalone as a cover in spread view.

Reading direction: Choose whether pages turn from left to right or right to left.

Document information: View PDF metadata such as title, author, subject, keywords, and PDF version.

Mouse Navigation: Enable clicking left/right edges for page turns.

Portrait single page: Choose whether single page view is used in portrait orientation.

Cover setting: Choose cover placement for each reading direction.

Reset: Reset app settings and close the current document.

Device Test: Run hardware and performance tests on your device.
